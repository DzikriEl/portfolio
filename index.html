01 — About

A short introduction

Hello, my name is Dzikri Faizziyan. I work at the intersection of data, research, and insight, with a background in Computer Science and a strong interest in understanding how technology, ideas, and communication shape society.

My experiences range from machine learning research and data analysis to project coordination, public speaking, and digital content development. Throughout my journey, I have been involved in academic research, organizational leadership, policy-related initiatives, and community-based literacy movements that focus on critical thinking and public discourse.

I believe data is not only about numbers, but also about people, behavior, narratives, and the ability to transform information into meaningful understanding. This perspective led me to explore various fields including machine learning, policy research, digital communication, and visual storytelling.

During my academic journey in Computer Science at Universitas Pakuan, I worked on several projects involving Natural Language Processing, survey-based research, and sentiment analysis using IndoBERT and INSET Lexicon. Beyond technical work, I actively participated in organizations, public discussions, leadership forums, and creative initiatives that strengthened my communication, analytical thinking, and collaborative skills.

Outside of research and analysis, I also have experience in managing communities, moderating discussions, speaking at educational forums, and creating digital visual content for social and educational movements.

Right now, I am interested in opportunities related to data analysis, research, policy, strategic communication, and multidisciplinary projects that combine technology with human-centered perspectives.
